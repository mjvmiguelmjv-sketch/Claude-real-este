## Video ad scripts

### AD-V-01 · TOFU · Fee-shock (workhorse)
- **0–1.5s:** Bold number on screen "2.9%+" → smash to "0.1%". VO: "Your processor is taking thousands you'll never see."
- **2–12s:** type the math: "$100k in sales = ~$2,900 in card fees. Or $100 with Zeno." Show dashboard fee line.
- **13–20s:** "30x less. Same sales. Instant settlement. No chargebacks."
- **CTA card:** "Calculate your savings → zenobank.io"

### AD-V-02 · TOFU · Chargebacks / frozen funds
- **0–1.5s:** "A customer can take your money back months later. Or your processor can just freeze it."
- **2–12s:** quick visualization of a chargeback + a 180-day hold. VO: "On-chain payments are final. Non-custodial means the money's in YOUR wallet."
- **13–20s:** "Un-reversible. Un-freezable. Yours."
- **CTA:** "Own your payments → zenobank.io"

### AD-V-03 · MOFU · 5-minute setup demo
- **0–1.5s:** screen-record, timer at 00:00. "Watch me add crypto checkout to a store in real time."
- **2–25s:** install plugin → paste API key → enable → test payment → "settled instantly."
- **26–30s:** timer < 5:00. "No code. No merchant account. Done."
- **CTA:** "Set up yours → zenobank.io"

### AD-V-04 · TOFU · Cross-border / any country
- **0–1.5s:** map/globe. "Selling abroad? Cards punish you for it."
- **2–12s:** "Cross-border + FX surcharges, plus declined foreign cards. Crypto rails: 0.1%, same in every country, instant."
- **13–20s:** "Mexico, Vietnam, the Netherlands — one checkout, one fee."
- **CTA:** "Get paid anywhere → zenobank.io"

### AD-V-05 · MOFU/BOFU · Proof / testimonial
- **0–1.5s:** merchant on cam (or quote card): "I was losing 3% on every sale."
- **2–15s:** 2–3 merchant snippets mapped to pains (fees, chargebacks, frozen funds) + dashboard b-roll. "300+ merchants. 5/5."
- **16–22s:** "Real businesses. Real receipts — on-chain."
- **CTA:** "Join them → zenobank.io"

### AD-V-06 · ALL STAGES (esp. BOFU) · Founder trust — THE differentiator
- **0–1.5s:** Hugo, face to camera, lower-third "Hugo Pino — Founder, Zeno Bank": "Every crypto payment company hides who runs it. I don't."
- **2–15s:** "I'm Hugo. I built Zeno in the open. My name's on it, you can hold your own keys, and every sale is recorded on-chain. In an industry full of frozen funds and anonymous teams, I think you deserve to know who's behind your money."
- **16–22s:** "0.1%, instant, no chargebacks — from a company you can actually look in the eye."
- **CTA:** "Start at zenobank.io. I'm Hugo — DM me."
- **Note:** competitors structurally can't run this ad. Test it everywhere; expect it to close.

### AD-V-07 · MOFU · Build-in-public / transparency
- **0–1.5s:** Hugo: "Most founders hide their numbers. I'll show you ours — here's why that matters for YOU."
- **2–15s:** show a real metric (merchants/uptime) + a shipped feature + one thing being fixed. "If I'll show you our numbers, you can trust how we handle yours."
- **16–20s:** "Built in public. Try it."
- **CTA:** "Follow the build → zenobank.io"

## Compliance checklist for every ad (must pass before publish)
- [ ] B2B/merchant (payment software) framing — not investing / "make money with crypto".
- [ ] No guaranteed returns, no "get rich," no fake urgency/scarcity.
- [ ] No tax-avoidance/evasion framing; any fiscal mention is educational + carries
      "not tax advice — consult a licensed professional."
- [ ] Claims backed (0.1%, instant settlement, no chargebacks, 300+ merchants, 5/5).
- [ ] Meta crypto-advertiser policy satisfied (approval/licensing on file) for targeted geos.
- [ ] `utm_content` = AD-ID; landing page matches the promise.
