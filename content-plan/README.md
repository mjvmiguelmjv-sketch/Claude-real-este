# Zeno Bank — Content Engine Kit

A ready-to-run organic + paid content system to attract crypto-fluent SME merchants
to Zeno Bank (zenobank.io), built around a single differentiator the competition
can't copy: **a visible, accountable founder — Hugo Pino — and radical transparency.**

## Who this is for
Crypto-aware small/medium businesses (e-commerce, SaaS, digital goods, cross-border
merchants) who already understand crypto and are bleeding margin to card fees,
chargebacks, FX, and processor freezes.

## Channels
- **Primary organic:** TikTok + Instagram (Reels-first, carousels for saves).
- **Support:** YouTube (Shorts repurposed + long-form for high-intent search).
- **Acquisition:** Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook).

## Non-negotiable: Compliance guardrails
Zeno is a legitimate gateway and **all funds received are declared.** Customer pains
about "taxes" and "the tax office" are addressed **only** through compliance-positive
education (how to declare crypto income correctly, keep audit-ready books). We **never**
frame Zeno as a tool to avoid or evade tax or to hide income. "No KYC" is sold strictly
as *fast, low-friction onboarding* — never as a way to stay invisible to authorities.
Fiscal content always carries a "not tax advice — consult a licensed advisor" note.
See the full checklist in `00-strategy-master.md` and apply it to every asset before publishing.

## Asset ID convention
Every script/asset has an ID (e.g. `SF-03`, `CR-02`, `AD-V-04`, `FV-05`) so the
calendar can reference real, executable pieces. IDs are stable — reuse them in your
scheduler and in UTM tags.
