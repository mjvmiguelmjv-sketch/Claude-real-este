# 03 — YouTube Suggestions (support channel)

## Role of YouTube
Two jobs: (1) **Shorts** as a third distribution surface for the same vertical clips
(low extra effort, big reach), and (2) **long-form** as the **high-intent search + deep-trust**
asset — the content people watch right before they sign up, and that ranks for years.
YouTube is where a hesitant merchant goes to verify "is Zeno legit and how does it actually work."

## Channel setup
- **Channel:** Zeno Bank. Handle @zenobank.
- **Banner:** "Accept Crypto at 0.1% · Instant Settlement · No Chargebacks · Built in public by Hugo Pino."
- **About:** clear value prop + founder name + links (dashboard, docs, demo, plugins).
- **Sections:** Shorts · How it works / Tutorials · Founder & Build-in-public · Merchant Stories · Compare.
- **Channel trailer:** a 60–90s Hugo intro ("Who I am, what Zeno is, why 0.1% and why transparency").

## Shorts strategy (volume, low effort)
- Repurpose every TikTok/Reel as a Short (vertical, <60s, subtitles burned in).
- Keyword the **title** (YT Shorts are searchable): e.g. "Accept USDT on Shopify (0.1% fee)".
- 1/day during ramp; Shorts feed new subscribers to the long-form.

## Long-form concepts (5–12 min) — the high-intent library
Prioritize titles that match what the ICP searches. Suggested first 10:

1. **"How to Accept Crypto Payments on Shopify (Step-by-Step, 0.1% Fee)"**
   — full screen-share setup with the plugin; the conversion workhorse.
2. **"WooCommerce Crypto Payments: Full Setup in Under 10 Minutes"**
   — same for Woo; pair with the WordPress plugin listing.
3. **"Crypto Payment Gateways Compared: Fees, Settlement & Custody (2026)"**
   — honest comparison incl. competitors; win trust by being fair. Zeno's edge: 0.1% + named founder + your-keys.
4. **"Stripe vs Crypto Checkout: The Real Cost of Card Fees at Scale"**
   — the margin-math video; spreadsheet on screen.
5. **"How to Stop Losing Money to Chargebacks (Permanently)"**
   — segment A/B pain; on-chain finality explained.
6. **"Custodial vs Non-Custodial: Who Actually Controls Your Money?"**
   — trust/education; positions the your-keys option.
7. **"How to Keep Clean, Audit-Ready Books on Crypto Sales"**
   — compliance-positive education. **On-screen + description disclaimer: not tax advice.**
8. **"I Built a Crypto Payment Company in Public — Here's Everything" (Hugo)**
   — the founder doc; the deep-trust asset. Numbers, mission, why transparency.
9. **"Accept International Payments Without Card Declines or FX Fees"**
   — segment C / cross-border.
10. **"Developer Guide: Integrate Crypto Checkout with the Zeno API/SDK"**
    — for the agency/dev multiplier (segment C); code on screen, links to GitHub examples & docs.

## Titles, thumbnails, SEO
- **Titles:** put the keyword + the number/benefit up front ("0.1%", "Step-by-Step", "in 10 Minutes").
- **Thumbnails:** big readable text (3–4 words), one visual (dashboard / fee comparison / Hugo's face), high contrast. Founder face on trust/story videos.
- **Description:** 2–3 keyword-rich sentences up top, timestamps/chapters, links (dashboard, docs, plugins, demo), and the fiscal disclaimer where relevant.
- **Chapters** on every long-form (watch-time + searchability).
- **End screens & cards:** point to the next logical video (setup → comparison → founder) and to the dashboard.
- **Pinned comment:** the CTA + link + "ask me anything" (Hugo replies).

## Keyword targets (research seeds)
"accept crypto payments", "crypto payment gateway", "accept USDT/USDC", "crypto checkout
Shopify/WooCommerce", "lowest fee crypto gateway", "stop chargebacks", "accept crypto
[country]", "crypto payment API", "stablecoin payments business".

## Cadence (realistic)
- **Shorts:** daily (repurposed).
- **Long-form:** 1/week to start (or 1 every 2 weeks if resource-constrained) — quality and
  searchability matter more than frequency here. Lead with videos #1, #2, #8 first.

## Note
YouTube is explicitly the **support** channel — don't let it slow the IG/TikTok engine.
The minimum viable YouTube presence = Shorts repurposing + the 3 priority long-forms
(#1 Shopify setup, #2 Woo setup, #8 founder story). Expand only once organic short-form is humming.
